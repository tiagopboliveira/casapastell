Labor Model & Staffing Plan

Operating Hours

8:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily (10 hours). Bakers start at 6:00 AM for morning prep. Two shifts overlap during peak lunch (11 AM – 2 PM).

Staffing Philosophy

Two distinct functions: bakers (back-of-house, skilled production) and FOH (front-of-house, service and POS). Bakers are not interchangeable with FOH. Founder runs floor management, scheduling, and quality control in Year 1; a dedicated GM is added in Year 2 alongside the second store.

G&A Structure

Year 1: founder manages single store directly (G&A = $0). Year 2+: founder salary moves to G&A plus corporate overhead for accounting, legal, ops management, and finance — scaling sub-linearly as stores grow.

Commissary Kitchen

Evaluate implementing a commissary kitchen model after 5 stores. A central production kitchen for dough prep and custard base could reduce per-store equipment needs and improve consistency — but only if the unit economics support it.

How Investors Get Paid: The 80/20 → 20/80 Waterfall + 8% Pref

Casa Pastell uses a pref-and-promote LLC waterfall. The total round size is $500,000 (matches the total capital required from the Ask section above). Investors receive their preferred return first, then 80% of remaining cash until 1× payback, then the split flips to 20%/80% in the operator's favor in perpetuity.
